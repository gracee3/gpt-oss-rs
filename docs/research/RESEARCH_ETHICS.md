# Research ethics and disclosure

## Authorship and responsibility

`gracee3` is the v0.1.0 release author and responsible maintainer. That role
includes the decision to publish, the claims made by the release, the selected
evidence, and correction or withdrawal of claims if later faults are found.

This repository originated as a narrowed fork of
[m0at/rvllm](https://github.com/m0at/rvllm). m0at and other contributors retain
explicit lineage credit through Git history, [NOTICE](../../NOTICE), and the
[third-party notices](../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). The publication does not
transfer or erase inherited authorship.

## AI assistance

OpenAI Codex agents assisted with source research, experimental and publication
drafting, implementation, command execution, and validation. The agents are
not authors, maintainers, evidence authorities, funders, or independent
replicators. Machine-generated analysis was accepted only through repository
review, executable checks, recorded artifacts, or explicit labeling as an
inference, limitation, or unverified proposal.

## Evidence and negative results

The release preserves failed gates, divergent results, slower candidates, and
incomplete work when they are relevant to interpretation. Evidence authority
belongs to the checked source identity, raw measurements, hashes, validation
logic, and ultimately the responsible release author—not to prose generated by
a human or an agent.

Local file paths, hostnames, hardware serials, credentials, private records,
and unrelated system details are excluded from publication artifacts. Model
and software identities are included only to the extent required to reproduce
the claims.

## Funding and interests

No external funding or sponsorship was declared for this release. No competing
interests were declared. OpenAI Codex use is disclosed as tooling assistance,
not funding, sponsorship, endorsement, or authorship.

## Scope of responsible use

This is experimental research software. It has not been validated for
high-stakes, safety-critical, regulated, or production service use. The model's
license and use policy remain independently applicable; this project does not
expand them.
